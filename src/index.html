05 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Can you improve our existing website?＋

Yes. We can review what you have and suggest focused improvements or a redesign, depending on your goals and the condition of the current setup.

Do we need to replace all our tools?＋

Usually, the starting point is the tools you already use. We look at what can connect reliably and only recommend changes where there is a practical reason.

How do you use AI in automation?＋

Where it helps with a specific task, such as sorting enquiries or drafting a response. We agree on data access, review steps, and how exceptions are handled before building.

Can you work under our agency’s brand?＋

Yes. We can support white-label delivery. We agree on responsibilities, communication, access, and handover so your client relationship stays clear.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?＋

That depends on the scope, existing systems, content, and integrations. After an initial conversation, we can outline the work, dependencies, and an estimated timeline and cost.
